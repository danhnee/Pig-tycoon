# Pig Tycoon UI/UX spec (uGUI, 16:9 landscape, PC + Android) — 3 Nov 2026

The user's direction (3 Nov): the controls must follow the best-rated game UIs, and every function must really work with the game logic. References studied:

- the Genshin quest log;
- Stardew storytelling and its clock/weather;
- the Star Rail phone menu;
- the Zelda single tracked quest;
- the Hades edge HUD;
- Obra Dinn's notebook;
- VN dialogue conventions;
- Material, Xbox and AGI accessibility guidelines.

Sources are listed per section. Sizes marked *proposed* are UX defaults, not balance numbers. Argon owns the look; this file fixes layout, information and behaviour.

## 0. Global rules

**Canvas and layout**
- Reference resolution 1920×1080, Scale With Screen Size, match = height.
- A single `SafeAreaRoot`; the app is locked to landscape.

**Touch and reach**
- Touch targets ≥ 48 dp with 8 dp gaps. That is about 120 px at the 1080 reference: primary buttons 140 px, secondary 112 px *proposed*. A hit box may be larger than the icon.
- Frequent actions go in the bottom-left and bottom-right thumb arcs. The top edge is for display and rare actions only. Nothing touchable within 24 px of an edge.

**Text**
- UI text ≥ 28 px at 1080p; dialogue ≥ 46 px, ≤ 40 characters per line.
- Every label sits on a plate with an outline, contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
- The font must render stacked Vietnamese diacritics.

**Meaning and entry points**
- Quality I–IV is shown by colour **and** numeral, never colour alone.
- One entry point per function:
  - PC: one key per panel.
  - Touch: secondary panels sit behind one "Phone/Notebook" button on the right, the Star Rail pattern.

## 1. HUD corner map

| Zone | Always | On demand |
|---|---|---|
| Top-left | portrait, guard bar (Chống Chịu), stamina (current, with the daily-load part as a dark locked segment), gold | level/EXP row for ~3 s when it changes |
| Top-centre | — | wave / pressure banner, zone name on entry |
| Top-right | time / period / weather widget, quest tracker below it | Sinh Cảnh chip when it changes or is low |
| Left edge | — | toast stack |
| Bottom-left | floating joystick (touch) | — |
| Bottom-centre | hotbar (5 touch / 8 PC), interaction prompt above it | — |
| Bottom-right | primary action button (140 px), Z/X/V/R on an arc, dodge + counter inside | tool-wheel button on the arc |
| Right edge | Phone/menu button | herd alert pill (sick, hungry, panic) |

Herd lists, forecast and notebook are not on the HUD; they live in menus.

## 2. Time / period / weather widget (top-right)

- **Rows:**
  - Day number and weekday.
  - Weather icon and period icon (Sáng/Trưa/Chiều/Tối).
  - A half-dial with 4 coloured sectors plus digital time.
- Weather and period never share a symbol.
- **Tap / Tab:** forecast card for today and tomorrow, with one plain line of effect per weather.
- **Period change:** the sector glows and a P3 toast names the period. In the last hour before Tối the needle pulses.

Sources: Stardew clock block; Dave the Diver day segments.

## 3. Quest tracker + journal (Genshin model)

**Journal (J / Phone → Nhiệm vụ)**
- Tabs: Chính / Phụ / Hằng ngày / Thân thiết, with collapsible chapters.
- Detail pane: title and place → **current step** (bold, n/m) → 1–2 sentences → rewards → **[Dẫn đường]**.
- Steps are checkboxes; finished steps are struck through.
- Colour bars: main gold, side blue, daily green, affinity pink.

**Tracker and pinning**
- One tracked quest, plus up to 2 pinned collapsed (Zelda BotW's single active marker).
- The tracker shows the type bar, name, current step, and an arrow with distance in tiles.
- It collapses to one line after 8 s without change. Tap opens the journal; T cycles quests.

**Navigation**
- An in-world beacon over the target; an edge arrow when it is off screen.
- The beacon hides within ~6 tiles.
- If the target is in another map, the arrow points to the exit.

Sources: genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Quest/Menu, /wiki/Quest; zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Log.

## 4. Tool wheel + hotbar

- **Hotbar:**
  - Keys 1–8, or tap a slot.
  - The active slot is raised and outlined; stack count bottom-right, quality top-left.
  - The tool in hand is visible on the character.
- **Wheel:**
  - Hold Tab/Q or hold the button, slide, release to select. Release in the centre cancels. A short tap swaps to the last tool.
  - ≤ 8 fixed segments. An unavailable tool is greyed out, never removed.
  - Time runs at 25 % while open, outside combat *proposed*.
- **Context:** standing at a trough highlights the feed bag. Never auto-switch the tool.

Sources: Steam radial menus; bigmedium radial-menus-for-touch.

## 5. Equipment / skills / engraving

**Equipment (C) — done 3 Nov in `CharacterPanel.BuildEquipment`**
- Paper-doll in the centre with 6 independent slots, 3 per side.
- Bag of owned pieces filtered by slot.
- Compare card with ±deltas against the worn piece; tap twice to wear; "Tiệm" only at the market.
- No set banners.

**Skills**
- Z/X/V/R strip.
- Tapping a skill opens a card: one sentence, cooldown, input icons.
- On the HUD: a radial cooldown sweep, then a flash and sound when ready.

**Engraving (Phù Văn / Mệnh Ấn)**
- Opened from an item card ("Khắc").
- Item and socket rings on the left; eligible runes/seals on the right; a before/after preview in the middle.
- Confirm names exactly what is consumed.
- Any "pick 1 of N" uses Hades-style cards.

## 6. Notebook "Đã thấy / Sự thật"

- Two pages: observed (handwritten) and confirmed (typeset).
- A confirmed fact animates from handwriting to ink, the Obra Dinn pattern.
- Tabs: Sự việc / Nhân vật.
- Every entry has "Xem lại" to replay its scene. New entries get a quill badge.
- Monologue arrives as margin notes, not popups.

## 7. Dialogue / VN scenes

- **Box and speakers:** the box takes the bottom ~28 % of the screen. The nameplate is tinted per speaker; the active portrait is bright and in front.
- **Text:** ≤ 3 lines, ≥ 46 px, typewriter at 40 ms per character (adjustable or instant). The first tap completes the line, the second advances; ▼ shows when the line is done.
- **Controls:** Lịch sử / Tự động / Bỏ qua. Skip covers read text only; Auto and Skip are exclusive.
  - PC keys: Space/Enter, A, Ctrl, L.
- **Choices:** buttons stacked on the right, ≥ 112 px.
- **Story delivery:** affinity scenes trigger on entering a place (Stardew heart events, ≤ 2 min). Letters go to the mailbox; an icon appears only when there is mail.

Sources: naninovel text printers; Stardew heart-event writing tips.

## 8. Toast priority (implemented channel: `ToastOverlay.Show`)

| Priority | Examples | Presentation |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | guard near 0, a pig dying, raid start | top-centre banner + sound; stays until handled or 6 s |
| P1 | pig sick or hungry, stamina low, storm coming | toast + icon + "Xem" jump; 5 s |
| P2 | quest step, affinity milestone, notebook entry | 4 s |
| P3 | pickups, gold ±, period change | merged ("+5 Cám"), 2.5 s, dropped on overflow |

- At most 3 visible on PC, 2 on phone.
- History of the last 30 under Phone → Thông báo.
- Hit feedback stays on the object, not in the feed.

## 9. Interaction prompt

One prompt, for the nearest valid target: "[E] Cho ăn" on PC; on touch the primary button's icon changes to match.

## 10. Herd, Habitat, Markets (Phone apps)

- **Herd:** cards with sprite, name and 4 icon meters (hunger, mood, health, growth). Problems sort first. Faces, not numbers (Moonlighter).
- **Habitat:** one gauge plus its 3 biggest contributors, each in a plain sentence.
- **Markets:**
  - Day market: buy/sell tabs with quantity steppers, plus "Bán hết loại này".
  - Night stall / auction: a bid card with a timer.
- **Build mode:** bottom tray, green/red ghost preview, confirm bottom-right, cancel bottom-left.

## 11. Do-not list

1. No text walls. Long text goes behind "Chi tiết".
2. No design jargon or internal ids (GDD, ADR, TẠM, "Ch1_Chon", formulas). Use `PlayerText.Clean` for Core messages.
3. No duplicate buttons for one function on one screen.
4. No set-bonus visuals.
5. No colour-only signals.
6. No frequent tappable controls in the top corners.
7. No open animation over 200 ms before the panel accepts input.
8. No modal popups for P2/P3.
9. No icon with two meanings.
10. Nothing under the notch or within 24 px of an edge.
11. No text below 28 px (46 px in dialogue).
12. No player time-speed controls (time passes by sleeping and resting; the dev panel only).
13. No mid-game protagonist switch.

## Verification gate

Every panel or widget delivery runs `tools/ui_probe.sh`:
- every control is tapped like a finger and its effect on Core, toasts, text and animation is recorded in `.agent_scratch/uiprobe.md`;
- one real in-game PNG is taken per panel (`uiprobe_panels/`).

Mockups do not count.
